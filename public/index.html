QUESTIONS

Before you order

Is it safe to pay by Vodafone Cash or InstaPay?

Yes. You place the order first, then you see the number and the exact amount to send. After you pay, you upload the screenshot. A real person checks every payment before the code is released.

What if the code doesn't work?

Message us on WhatsApp with your order number and a screenshot of the error. We replace the code or refund you. See the refund and replacement policy above.

How fast is delivery?

Usually 5 to 15 minutes after we confirm your payment. Send the screenshot as soon as you pay and we check it right away.

Do I upload the screenshot before or after I pay?

After. Place your order, send the money, then come back to the same page and upload the screenshot, or send it on WhatsApp with your order number already filled in.

How long is my order held?

Your codes are reserved for a limited time while you pay. The exact time left is shown on your order page.

I sent the wrong amount. What now?

Message us on WhatsApp right away with your order number and the screenshot. We will sort it out with you.
